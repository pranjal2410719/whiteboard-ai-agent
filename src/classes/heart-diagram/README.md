## 1. Class Overview & Learning Objectives
Students master the anatomy and mechanics of the human cardiovascular double circulation system:
1. Identify the 4 heart chambers (Right Atrium, Right Ventricle, Left Atrium, Left Ventricle).
2. Explain the function of the 4 valves (Tricuspid, Pulmonary, Mitral/Bicuspid, Aortic).
3. Trace the pathway of Pulmonary and Systemic circulation.
4. Understand the Cardiac Cycle stages (Atrial Systole, Ventricular Systole, Joint Diastole).
