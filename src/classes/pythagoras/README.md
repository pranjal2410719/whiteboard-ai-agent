## 1. Class Overview & Learning Objectives
Students master the Pythagorean Theorem:
1. Identify the right angle ($90^\circ$) and the two perpendicular legs ($a$ and $b$).
2. Locate the hypotenuse ($c$) opposite the right angle.
3. Apply $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$ to calculate hypotenuse length (e.g. $3\text{ m}$ and $4\text{ m} \to 5\text{ m}$).
4. Rearrange the theorem to calculate a missing leg ($b^2 = c^2 - a^2$).
